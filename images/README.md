## 1. The VLSI Visual Identity Constitution

> ### ⚡ The Ironclad Identity Rule:
> **Every generated visual must be recognizably related to VLSI, semiconductor engineering, digital hardware, ASIC, FPGA, RTL, verification, DFT, or chip physical design. If the image could equally belong to a generic software hackathon, IT MUST BE REJECTED.**

### What Is BANNED:
- ❌ **No generic software hackathon tropes:** No laptops with random code on screens, no developers wearing hoodies, no coffee cup cliches.
- ❌ **No sci-fi cyborgs or humanoid robots:** No generic AI robot faces or fantasy androids.
- ❌ **No dark cyberpunk rooms or neon glow blobs:** No blue/purple blurry mesh backgrounds.
- ❌ **No generic clipart motherboards:** No green toy PCBs with random wandering glowing lines.
- ❌ **No illegible AI text:** Use `--no text, numbers, watermarks` to prevent garbled pseudo-English.

### What Is ENFORCED:
- ✅ **Authentic Semiconductor Motifs:** Exposed silicon dies, multi-tier copper interconnects (M1-M8), wire-bond micro-ribbons, step-and-repeat wafer lithography, photolithography alignment marks, test-element groups (TEG).
- ✅ **Physical Design & Verification:** GDSII layout strata, clock tree synthesis (CTS) H-trees, power distribution networks (PDN), timing closure eye-diagrams, differential probe tips.
- ✅ **Hardware Realism:** Flip-chip BGA ceramic packages, high-bandwidth differential SMA probes, precision cleanroom test fixtures.
- ✅ **Light Mode Integration:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` seamless studio backgrounds, high contrast, razor-sharp focus, architectural contact shadows, museum-grade elegance.
