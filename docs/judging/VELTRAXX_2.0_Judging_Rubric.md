# VELTRAXX 2.0 - Single-Round Onsite Hackathon Judging Overview

*Prepared for Human Evaluators — Department of Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology), SIET Coimbatore*

## Evaluation Format
- **Event Structure:** Single-round, 24-hour continuous onsite hackathon (December 15-16, 2026).
- **Eligibility:** Open to all academic disciplines across UG, PG, and research scholars (strictly 3 members per team, maximum 60 teams).
- **No Preliminary Round:** There is no Round 1 PPT screening or online shortlisting.
- **Verification Focus:** Teams are evaluated live onsite on engineering execution, original hardware/software functionality, demonstration of working outputs, and technical clarity across Problem Statements `VTX-T1-01` through `VTX-T5-05`.

*Note: Specific judging criterion weightings and scoring sheets are finalized and issued directly by the SIET organizing committee prior to event kick-off.*
