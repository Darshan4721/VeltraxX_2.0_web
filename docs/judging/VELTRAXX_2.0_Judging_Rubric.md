# VELTRAXX 2.0 - Official Round 1 & Round 2 Judging Rubric

*Prepared for Human Evaluators & Authorized Gemini-Assisted Screening Workspace (`02_Official_Evaluation_Rubrics`)*

## Round 1: Online Blueprint Evaluation Rubric (100 Points)

All Round 1 submissions are evaluated strictly by **Problem Statement Code** (`VTX-T1-01` through `VTX-T5-05`) and calibrated within each team's **Academic Tier** (`Tier A`: UG 1st/2nd Year vs `Tier B`: UG 3rd/4th Year, PG, Research Scholars).

| Criterion | Weight | Key Signals Evaluated from Confirmed PDF |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Problem Decomposition & Engineering Rigor** | 25% | Clarity of constraints, physical/architectural assumptions, edge cases, and quantitative targets |
| **2. System / Silicon Architecture & Feasibility** | 30% | Modularity of block diagrams, timing/power/protocol realism, hardware-software boundary clarity |
| **3. Innovation & Originality** | 20% | Novel architectural trade-offs beyond textbook boilerplate, verifiable citations/datasheets |
| **4. Implementation Readiness (24H Finale Scope)** | 15% | Realistic 24-hour MVP scope, simulation/prototyping tool readiness, BYOD feasibility |
| **5. Slide Compliance & Clarity** | 10% | Strict adherence to official template structure, `<= 8` slides, clear diagrams |

### Hard Compliance Checks
- Valid PDF export (`<= 3 MB`, `1..8` pages).
- Correct `problem_code` alignment between team registration and PDF blueprint.
- Zero plagiarized unattributed blocks.

## Round 2: 24-Hour Offline Finale Judging Rubric (100 Points)

- **Core Build (50 Points):** Base engineering fidelity, live waveform / hardware / working prototype demonstration.
- **50/50 Live Engineering Twist Defense (50 Points):** Architecture resilience and adaptation to surprise constraints injected mid-sprint.
