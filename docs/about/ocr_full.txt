KAVYA M A SELF-TESTING, FAULT-TOLERANT CAN CONTROLLER The CAN protocol defines five error checks — bit, stuff, CRC, form and acknowledgement — but these detect only communication-level faults. This article presents a twelve-module CAN controller with a Built-In Self-Test (BIST) engine using an LFSR test-pattern generator and MISR response analyzer to detect internal faults at power-up, reset and bus-idle periods.
