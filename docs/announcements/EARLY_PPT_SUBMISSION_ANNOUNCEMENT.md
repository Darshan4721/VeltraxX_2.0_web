**Subject / Title:** VELTRAXX 2.0: Round 1 PPT Submission Portal Now Open Early (Deadline Remains Dec 8, 11:59 PM IST)

Hello Participants & Teams,

To help teams avoid last-minute upload congestion and give everyone more time to finalize and verify their blueprints, we have **opened the Round 1 PPT Submission Portal early** at:

`https://veltrax-x-2-0-web.vercel.app/submit-ppt`

### Key Updates & Instructions
1. **Immediate Access:** You can upload your presentation PDF starting **immediately** instead of waiting for Dec 7.
2. **Final Deadline Unchanged:** All Round 1 submissions close strictly on **08 December 2026 at 11:59:59 PM IST**.
3. **Free Replacements Before Deadline:** Uploaded early and want to polish your slides later? You can **safely replace your PDF anytime before Dec 8, 11:59 PM IST** using your Team ID and Team Passcode. Your previous confirmed version stays safe unless your new upload succeeds completely.
4. **File Rules:**
   - **Format:** Exported `.pdf` only (no `.pptx` or Word files).
   - **Maximum Slides:** Strictly **8 slides maximum**.
   - **Maximum File Size:** Strictly **under 3 MB**.
5. **Credentials & Official Receipt:**
   - Registration (`₹0`) immediately issues your **Team ID** and private **Team Passcode** on screen and in your downloadable registration slip.
   - Upon successful PPT upload, you will receive an immediate **Submission Receipt ID** (`VTX2-XXX-V1-...`) which you can re-verify anytime on `/submit-ppt`.
   - *Note:* Email confirmation is supplementary - always save your registration slip and submission receipt directly from the website.
